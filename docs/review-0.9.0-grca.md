## Points to check (decided by Claude while building)

- **Effective dates of the IIA documents**: none of the files states one; publication dates are recorded. If you have the official effective dates, give them and I will add them.
- **COSO ERM English titles** are CRGO translations of the French summary; the official English list was not in the files.
- **ISO previews**: only headings and scope text were available; paraphrases say no more than the headings imply.
- **Scales chosen by CRGO** (evidence quality, deficiency severity, finding significance, engagement rating): no common scale exists in the documents held.
- **SP 800-53A**: two unlabelled SI-2(7) objectives got derived identifiers; method notations use the 800-53 label because seven catalogue labels repeat.
- **DSS contract**: the specification's `modelVersion: "TBD"` and `organizationId: "org-demo"` are refused by design.
- **MediBec** keeps its `w3id.org/crgo/data/medibec/` IRIs (grandfathered).
- **Assumption's alt label "hypothesis"** (approved in Sprint 1) now sits next to the new class *analytical hypothesis*; I suggest removing that alt label (a change to an approved term, so your call).
